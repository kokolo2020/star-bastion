# Star Bastion artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, using the user's two Space Tower Attack reference images for color, fortress design and swarm composition. Display title is STAR BASTION; subtitle is DEFEND TOGETHER.

Files:
- `star-bastion-icon-master.png`: square text-free fortress artwork.
- `icon-512-v2.png`, `icon-192-v2.png`: exported app icon sizes (regular icons, not maskable).
- `star-bastion-banner-master.png`: wide master key art.
- `star-bastion-thumbnail-1280x720.jpg`: compressed wide promotional/landing graphic.
- `../../img/tower0.png` through `tower4.png`: 256px transparent Pulse, Cannon, Laser, Frost and Rail sprites, matched white/navy armor and class-colored emitters, pointing upward.

Prompt specifications:
- Icon: premium square sci-fi cooperative fortress defense icon; one dominant white/navy fortress with cyan hexagonal reactor and three turrets, violet cosmic sky, orange enemy triangles, no text or UI.
- Banner: wide 16:9 game key art matching the icon fortress, cyan lasers versus a large orange triangular swarm, dramatic ringed-planet setting; exact title STAR BASTION and subtitle DEFEND TOGETHER; no UI or unrelated branding.
- Turrets: one isolated top-down upward-facing turret per image, transparent background, beveled white/navy armor, clean silhouette readable at small size; cyan twin-barrel Pulse, orange heavy Cannon, green energy Laser, icy blue Frost projector, magenta twin-rail Rail turret; no ground, labels, interface or projectiles.

Masters are preserved; sips was used only for deterministic resizing and JPEG exports. Previous root-level icon files are retained. Game art is decorative and does not change weapon costs, damage, hitboxes or multiplayer ownership rules.
